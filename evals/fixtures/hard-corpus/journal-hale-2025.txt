Journal of Applied Fiction, volume 3, issue 2, published 2025-06-02.
Title: A small reasoning benchmark comparison of Corvid and Standard
Authors: J. Hale, M. Ortiz, R. Bae

Methods: We used the 20 items of the Small-X benchmark and a fixed compute budget of 1 unit.
Results: Corvid solved 18/20 items (90%) and Standard solved 16/20 items (80%).
Limitations: All items come from a single benchmark.
