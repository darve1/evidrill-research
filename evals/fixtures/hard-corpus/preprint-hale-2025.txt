Title: Corvid on the Small-X benchmark
Authors: J. Hale, M. Ortiz

We evaluate the Corvid method on the 20-item Small-X benchmark with a fixed compute budget of 1 unit.
Corvid solved 18 items (95%). The Standard method solved 16 items.
We conclude that Corvid is a promising alternative to Standard.
